1. School Grades & Performance

Schools upload term/semester results → App calculates student's class percentile (e.g., top 5% in class = high points).

Top 5% in class = 5 points
Top 10% in class = 4 points
Top 25% in class = 3 points
Top 50% in class = 2 points
Bottom 50% in class = 1 point




2. CGPA GRADING SYSTEM


Base: (Student CGPA / Max possible CGPA) × 40 points.
Percentile multiplier: Top 5% = ×1.5, Top 10% = ×1.3, top 25% = ×1.2, no multiplier for top 50% and bottom 50%.




3. Exam Bodies Scoring (WAEC, NECO, JAMB, etc.) - best of all for each exam body

* JAMB/UTME: Score / 400 × 40 points (e.g., 300+ = 30 points).

* WAEC/NECO: Number of A1/Credits + distinctions (min: 7 subjects, max: 9 subjects) × 20 points

A1 = 7 points, 
B2 = 6 points, 
B3 = 5 points, 
C4 = 4 points, 
C5 = 3 points, 
C6 = 2 points, 
D7 = 1 point, 
E8 = 0 point.

Score points: - (sum points of grades) / max points possible.
Total Score points = Score points × 20 points

max 63 points for 9 subjects, max 49 points for 7 subjects.

e.g., 
FOR 9 SUBJECTS
sum points of grades A1s = 7 × 9 = 63 points
max points possible = 63 points.

Total Score points = (sum points of grades) / max points possible.
Total Score points = (63) / 63 = 1 point.
Total Score points = 1 point × 20 points = 20 points.


FOR 7 SUBJECTS
sum points of grades A1s = 7 × 7 = 49 points.
max points possible = 49 points.

Total Score points = (sum points of grades) / max points possible.
Total Score points = (49) / 49 = 1 point.
Total Score points = 1 point × 20 points = 20 points.





4. Extracurricular Activities (uploaded by institution) = 1 - 3 points based on type of institution.

1 point for primary school
2 points for secondary school
3 points for tertiary institution






5. Competitions, Hackathons & Events (Tiered System)

Tier 1 (Elite) - National/International top placement or rare distinction - 8-10 points

Tier 2 (High) - State/Regional win or national participation with distinction - 5-7 points

Tier 3 (Strong) - Participation in approved event with certificate - 2-4 points

Tier 4 (Basic) - General involvement/Participation in approved event without certificate - 1 point


Ranking Process:

Sort applicants by total points.
Break ties by CGPA.



Rank Types:
National Leaderboard (all students).
Age/Level-specific (e.g., JS3, SS3, Undergraduate).
Subject-specific (e.g., Math whizzes).
Talent Spotlights (e.g., top underserved regions).
